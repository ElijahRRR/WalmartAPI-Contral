## Overview

Products subject to our policy on Alcohol must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) including our policy on [Food Products](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Food-products) or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

## What is the policy?

Walmart prohibits the sale of most alcoholic beverages and products on its Marketplace. In addition, federal law strictly prohibits individuals from producing distilled spirits at home, therefore, such products are also prohibited from sale on Walmart Marketplace.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is considered an alcoholic product?

While alcohol’s definition can vary between state and local laws, alcohol is typically considered any product that contains not less than one-half of one percent (0.5%) of alcohol by volume and is intended for human consumption.
