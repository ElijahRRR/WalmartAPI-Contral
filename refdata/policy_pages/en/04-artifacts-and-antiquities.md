## Prohibited Products Policy: Artifacts and Antiquities

**In this guide:**

### Overview

Products subject to our policy on Artifacts and Antiquities must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) including our policy on [Autographs and Collectibles](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Autographs-and-collectibles) and [Native American Products](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-products-policy:-native-american-products) or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

### What is the policy?

Historical artifacts, antiquities and antiques are prohibited from sale on Walmart’s Marketplace.

### Policy basics

| **Prohibited** | **Allowed** |
| --- | --- |
| - Artifacts<br>- Fossils<br>- Geological formations<br>- Relics<br>- Remains<br>- Sacred and tribal items<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Any item that claims a tribe name and/or to be “Indian” and is **not** produced by someone other than tribe member or certified Indian artisan<br>- Any item that came from Native American land<br>- Historical grave markers<br>- Burial items<br>- Antiques, e.g. used products | - Books about artifacts<br>- Reproduction pieces of fossils or other artifacts when clearly indicated as such |
