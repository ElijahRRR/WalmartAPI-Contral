### Overview

Products subject to our policy on Auto and Motor Vehicles must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

### What is the policy?

Walmart prohibits the sale of cars and restricts the sale of other motor vehicles on its Marketplace. In addition, Walmart does not allow the sale of car parts and other accessories that are illegal, do not meet regulatory requirements, or that are designed to defeat, interfere, or bypass motor vehicle safety standards or environmental controls.
