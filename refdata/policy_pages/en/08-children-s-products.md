## Overview

Products subject to our policy on Children’s Products must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) including our policy on [Baby Products](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Baby-products) or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

## What is the policy?

All sellers on Walmart’s Marketplace are required to ensure that [children’s products](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/Childrens-Products) comply with all applicable federal, state and local laws. Children’s products are generally defined as consumer products designed or intended primarily for children 12 years of age or younger.

In accordance with federal regulations, domestic manufacturers and importers of children’s products must certify, in a written Children’s Product Certificate (CPC), based on test results from a third-party Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC)-accepted laboratory, that their children’s products comply with all applicable children’s product safety rules.

Sellers must provide copies of a valid CPC and the corresponding third-party test report for each children’s product listed on Walmart’s Marketplace.

Sellers may list children’s products on Walmart’s Marketplace only if each product is accompanied by a valid CPC and a corresponding third-party test report issued by a CPSC-accepted lab.

When requested, sellers must upload CPCs and test reports for each covered item in Seller Center using the [**Item compliance**](https://seller.walmart.com/item-compliance) widget on the [**Health and Compliance**](https://seller.walmart.com/settings/account/health-compliance) page.

### Additional information

Upon request, sellers must ensure that certificates are available to the Commission, Customs authorities, distributors and retailers as required by law and respond to outreach from government agencies and Walmart representatives in a timely manner.

**The CPC must be:**

- Written in English.
- Based on test results from a third-party [CPSC-accepted laboratory](https://www.cpsc.gov/cgi-bin/labsearch/).
- Contain all required elements and information as specified by the CPSC's CPC requirements, including:
  1. Identification of the product covered by the certificate.
  2. Citation to each CPSC children's product safety rule to which the product is being certified.
  3. Identification of the importer or domestic manufacturer certifying compliance, including name, full mailing address and telephone number.
  4. Contact information for the individual maintaining records of test results, including name, e-mail address, full mailing address and the telephone number.
  5. Date (month and year at minimum) and place (including city and state, country or administrative region) where the product was manufactured.
  6. Date and place (including city and state, country or administrative region) where the product was tested for compliance.
  7. Identification of the third-party laboratory on whose testing the certificate depends, including name, full mailing address and telephone number.

Visit the [CPSC's website](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Testing-Certification/Childrens-Product-Certificate) for additional information on CPC requirements and templates.

**Third-party test reports must be:**

- Written in English.
- Based on test results from a third-party CPSC-accepted laboratory.
- Show that the product complies with all applicable state and federal children's product laws and rules.

## Frequently asked questions

### My product is not a children's product, why do I need to submit a Children’s Product Certificate (CPC)?

It's possible that your listing qualifies as a children's product under Walmart's policy. If you believe an item isn't a children's product, select the *Help* button in the [Seller Center](https://seller.walmart.com/home) menu bar to contact Support and describe your issue using keywords COMP and CPC.

### How long will it take for my submissions to be reviewed?

Documents go through multiple stages of review that could take up to two weeks. Most documents are reviewed within three days. If your document status doesn't change within three to four weeks, select the *Help* button in the Seller Center menu bar to contact Support.

### What standards should my products be tested to?

Walmart requires compliance with all applicable state, federal and local standards. Please visit the [Consumer Product safety Commission's (CPSC) website](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Testing-Certification/Lab-Accreditation/Rules-Requiring-Third-Party-Testing) for clarity on which federal standards apply.

### There are minor differences between the SKU on the CPC certificate and the SKU in the backend (e.g., differences in packaging). Will this affect the review?

The description in Walmart's system must reasonably match the description in the CPC. Your documents may be rejected if there's no clear connection between the item described in the document and the Marketplace listing.

### The CPC requirements stipulate that the importer's address must be in English. Will it have an impact if the certificate applicant's address is in Pinyin?

The address must be provided in a way which would allow a reviewer to locate the facility on a map. An address listed entirely in Pinyin often cannot be used to locate a facility.

### My item was unpublished for violating the Children’s Product Policy, but it didn’t appear in the Item compliance hub. How can I get my item republished?

The items listed on the Seller Center **Item compliance** hub are those that will be impacted by enforcements. If you believe an item is at risk, but it does not show up in the **Item compliance** hub, please file a ticket under My Cases in Seller Center using the keywords COMP and CPC.

### Will I be advised of the rejection reason in Seller Center if my documents enter the Needs Action status?

If you have questions about document rejection, select the *Help* button in the Seller Center menu bar to contact Support and describe your issue using keywords COMP and CPC.

### I encountered an error while uploading the CPC file and was unable to successfully upload it. How should I proceed?

First, ensure that two separate documents were submitted as the tool won't accept the same document twice. You can also select the *Help* button in the seller Center menu bar to contact Support. Include a screenshot of the error if possible.

### Should the importer information be the U.S. agent's details, or can it also be the information of the manufacturer outside the U.S.?

Walmart requires the CPC to be prepared by the manufacturer or the domestic (U.S.) importer. A document prepared by a manufacturer outside the U.S. will be accepted.

### Are batch testing of similar products or sample testing of materials recognized?

Yes. Ensure that all products covered by the CPC and test report are adequately described so the connection can be made to the Walmart listings. If sample testing is appropriate under federal, state and local requirements, submit the available testing.
