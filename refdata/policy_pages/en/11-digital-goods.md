## Overview

Products subject to our policy on Digital Goods must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) including our policy on [Electronics](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Electronics-and-radio-frequency-devices) and [Software](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-products-policy:-software) or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

## What is the policy?

Digital goods and digitally delivered products are prohibited from sale on Walmart Marketplace. Digital goods are products that are readily available in digital form and are generally received through email, downloaded from the internet or accessed by logging into an online portal.
