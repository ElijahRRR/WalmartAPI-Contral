### Overview

Products subject to our policy on Funeral Products must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) including our policy on [Artifacts and Antiquities](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Artifacts-and-antiquities) or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

### What is the policy?

Walmart prohibits the sale of certain funeral-related products on its Marketplace.
