## Overview

Products subject to our policy on General-Use Products must also adhere to all Prohibited Products Policies.

## What is the policy?

This policy applies to all general-use consumer products subject to the Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC)-enforced safety rules, bans, standards or regulations. Children's products are excluded from this policy and instead require a Children's Product Certificate (CPC). For a full list of regulated product categories, refer to the CPSC Regulated Products List.

When selling on Walmart Marketplace, you must ensure that all covered products comply with all applicable federal, state and local laws and provide a valid General Certificate of Conformity (GCC), as described in the Consumer Product Safety Act (CPSA) and accompanying regulations for each covered item. The GCC must certify that the subject product complies with all applicable consumer product safety rules, bans, standards and regulations enforced by the CPSC, and the certification must be based on a test of each product or a reasonable testing program.

When requested, you must upload GCCs for each covered item through Seller Center. Certificates must also be made available to the CPSC, customs authorities, distributors, retailers and Walmart representatives upon request. To identify which items are available for GCC upload and to provide the requested documents, visit the Health and compliance page in Seller Center, then select Take action to be redirected to the Item compliance hub.

### Additional information

A valid GCC must be:

- Written in English.
- Based on a test of each product or a reasonable testing program.
- Inclusive of all required elements as specified by CPSC GCC requirements, including:
  - Identification of the product covered by the certificate.
  - Citation to each CPSC product safety regulation or statutory requirement to which the product is being certified.
  - Identification of the importer or domestic manufacturer certifying compliance, including name, full mailing address and telephone number.
  - Contact information for the individual maintaining records of test results, including name, email address, full mailing address and telephone number.
  - Date (month and year at a minimum) and place (including city and state, country, or administrative region) where the product was manufactured; if more than one location in the same city, include the street address.
  - Date and place (including city and state, country, or administrative region) where the product was tested for compliance.
  - Identification of any third-party laboratory that conducted testing, including name, full mailing address, and telephone number (If testing was conducted through an in‑house testing program rather than a third‑party laboratory, the certificate must identify the internal testing facility and describe the reasonable testing program used).
