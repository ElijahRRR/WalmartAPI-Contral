## Overview

Products subject to the Home Goods policy must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview), as applicable, on [Baby Products](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Baby-products) and [Recalled Products](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-products-policy:-recalled-products).

## What is the policy?

Walmart prohibits from sale on its marketplace any products, including home products, that are considered unsafe by a regulatory body such as the Federal Trade Commissions (FTC), Consumer Products Safety Commission (CPSC), and/or otherwise don’t comply with local, state, or federal regulation. In addition, items making improper or unsubstantiated product claims are also prohibited.
