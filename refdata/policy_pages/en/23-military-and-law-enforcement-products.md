## Prohibited Products Policy: Military and Law Enforcement Products

### Overview

Products subject to our policy on Military and Law Enforcement Products must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) including [Firearms](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Firearms), [Firearm Accessories](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Firearm-accessories) and [Knives](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Knives-and-other-melee-weapons) or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

### What is the policy?

Products designed and intended for use by law enforcement agencies and their employees are prohibited from sale on Walmart Marketplace.
