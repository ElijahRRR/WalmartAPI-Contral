### Overview

Products subject to our policy on Native American Products must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) including our policy on [Artifacts and Antiquities](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Artifacts-and-antiquities) or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

### What is the policy?

Walmart prohibits sellers from listing or selling any item in a manner that falsely suggests it is Native American, produced by an American Indian, or the product of a particular tribe or arts and crafts organization within the United States.

Products sold as Indian, Native American, or the product of a particular Indian Tribe, must be made by an enrolled member of a federally or officially State recognized Tribe, or a certified nonmember Indian artisan by the Tribe of their direct descent.

The use of a name or other Native American references must not infringe on any intellectual property rights of any tribe.
