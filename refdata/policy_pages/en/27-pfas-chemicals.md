## What is the policy?

Covered Products containing intentionally added PFAS chemicals are restricted from being sold on Walmart Marketplace in the states restricting their sale. “Intentionally added PFAS chemicals” means PFAS chemicals that a manufacturer has intentionally added to a product and that have a functional or technical effect on the product.

PFAS, also known as per- and poly-fluoroalkyl substances (including PFOS, PFOA and PTFE), are synthetic chemical substances widely used in industrial and consumer products since the 1940s (collectively, “PFAS chemicals”). These chemicals provide properties such as greaseproofing; waterproofing; water repellence; stick-proofing and stain, grease, oil and water resistance.

Due to their persistence in the environment, PFAS chemicals are sometimes referred to as “forever chemicals”. A number of states have enacted laws restricting intentionally added PFAS in certain product categories (the “Covered Products”). Below are some examples of Covered Products, but note that this list is not intended to be exhaustive. If you have any questions about whether your products are covered by any PFAS laws, consult with your legal counsel.

**Notes:**

Covered Products vary by state and may be updated periodically. You are expected to stay informed and up to date on evolving laws and regulations pertaining to PFAS, including the Covered Products in scope.
