## Overview

Products subject to our policy on [Resold](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Item%20setup/Resold/resold-program-overview) products must also adhere to other [Prohibited Product Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

## What is the policy?

The sale of products not in new condition is strictly prohibited. You’re only permitted to list products that are Restored, Pre-Owned, Open box or Remanufactured if you’ve been invited to participate in the Resold program. These items must meet all of the [condition, category, quality and disclosure requirements](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Item%20setup/Resold/Item-condition-types).

If you haven’t been invited, you must only list products in new condition. Any misrepresentation of condition or unapproved category listing is prohibited from sale on Walmart Marketplace.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I join the Resold program?

Participation in the Resold program is by invitation only, and not available to all Marketplace sellers. If you’d like to sell [Pre-Owned](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Item%20setup/Resold/resold-program-item-setup) items through the Resold program, visit the [**Exclusives programs**](https://seller.walmart.com/growth/exclusive-programs) page in Seller Center to apply.
