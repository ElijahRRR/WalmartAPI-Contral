**Overview**

Products subject to our policy on Restricted/Illegal Products must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

## Restricted/illegal products

## What is the policy?

Sellers are prohibited from listing any product for sale in any location where that product violates applicable federal, state, or local law or regulation.

## Policy basics

| **Prohibited** | **Allowed** |
| --- | --- |
| - Any product that is age-restricted by law.<br>- Any product that violates applicable laws and/or regulations.<br>- Any product encouraging or enabling illegal activity or circumvention of law. | - Products that can’t be sold in all U.S. states under applicable laws (*see State Restrictions section below for additional information). |

## State restrictions

## What is the policy?

Walmart generally prohibits sellers from listing any product that is not allowed to be sold in all U.S. states. In some product categories, however, Walmart allows you to designate specific states in which your products may be sold. In these categories, you’re responsible for providing Walmart with accurate information about the states in which your products may legally be sold.

As a seller, you’re responsible for (1) compliance with applicable federal, state, and local laws that apply in each jurisdiction in which your product is offered for sale, (2) obtaining any required permits or licenses, and (3) any penalties resulting from noncompliance. Walmart reserves the right, but doesn’t have the responsibility, to impose additional state restrictions in its discretion.
