### Overview

Products subject to our policy on Software must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) including [Digital Goods](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Digital-goods) or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

## What is the policy?

Software products that are illegal or infringe on another party’s intellectual property rights are prohibited from sale on Walmart Marketplace. Only full retail versions of software may be sold.
