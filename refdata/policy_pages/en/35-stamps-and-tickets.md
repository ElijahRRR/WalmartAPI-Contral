> **Overview**
>
> Products subject to our policy on United States Postal Service (USPS) stamps and Tickets must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) and applicable federal, state and local laws and regulations, or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

### United States Postal Service (USPS) stamps

### What is the policy?

The sale of USPS postage stamps is restricted for approved sellers only. Non-postage stamp collectibles and reference materials are allowed.

### Policy basics

| **Prohibited** | **Allowed with restriction** | Allowed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| - USPS stamps sold by unapproved sellers | - USPS stamps sold by approved sellers | - USPS stamp collecting books<br>- USPS stamp guidebooks<br>- Decorative stamps |

### Tickets

### What is the policy?

Digital and physical tickets to events of any kind are prohibited from being sold on Walmart Marketplace. Items that resemble tickets but are not used for entry to events are allowed.

### Policy basics

| Prohibited | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| - Lottery tickets<br>- e-Tickets<br>- Tickets for sporting or other entertainment events | - Raffle ticket rolls<br>- Work order tickets<br>- Fake lottery tickets<br>- Ticket memorabilia |
