## Air powered guns, BB guns and ammunition

## What is the policy?

Walmart prohibits the sale of certain air powered guns and BB guns and related ammunition on its Marketplace. For purposes of this policy, air powered guns include air guns, air rifles, and airsoft guns.

## Toy guns and imitation firearms

## What is the policy?

Walmart prohibits the sale of any toy, look-alike, or imitation firearms, which can include certain gel and water bead blasters and water guns, that do not contain or have markings affixed to the device required by federal, state, or local laws (collectively, “Imitation Firearms"). All toy guns, including gel and water bead blasters and water guns, listed for sale on Walmart’s Marketplace must comply with federal, state, and local laws.
