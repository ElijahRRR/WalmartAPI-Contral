### Overview

### What is the policy?

Firearms and their component parts are prohibited from sale on Walmart Marketplace. For purposes of this policy, the term "Firearms" include any weapon (including a starter gun) which is designed to or may readily be converted to expel a projectile by the action of an explosive, the frame or receiver of any such weapon. "Firearm Component Parts" include any element or replacement element specifically designed for a firearm and essential to its operation, including a barrel, frame or receiver, slide or cylinder, bolt or breech block, and any device designed or adapted to diminish the sound caused by firing a firearm such as silencers/suppressors.
