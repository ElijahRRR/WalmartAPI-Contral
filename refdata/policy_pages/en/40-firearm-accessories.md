**Prohibited Products Policy: Firearm accessories**

## Overview

Products subject to our policy on Firearm Accessories must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) including our policy on [Firearms](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Firearms), [Firearm Ammunition](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Firearm-ammunition), and [Game Guns](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Air-powered-guns,-BB-guns,-toy-guns-and-imitation-firearms) or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

## What is the policy?

In general, firearm accessories not strictly used for hunting purposes, are prohibited on Walmart Marketplace. For the purpose of this policy, "Firearm Accessories" means any item that is used in conjunction with or mounted on a firearm but is not essential to the basic function of the firearm. The term includes magazines, holsters, targets, and bulletproof gear.
