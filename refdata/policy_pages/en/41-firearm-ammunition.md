## Overview

Products subject to our policy on Firearm Ammunition must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) including our policy on [Firearms](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Firearms) and [Firearm Accessories,](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Firearm-accessories) and [Game Guns](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Air-powered-guns,-BB-guns,-toy-guns-and-imitation-firearms) or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

## What is the policy?

Firearm ammunition and most components are prohibited from sale on Walmart Marketplace. For the purposes of this policy, “ammunition" includes cartridge cases, primers, bullets, or propellant powder designed for use in any firearm.
