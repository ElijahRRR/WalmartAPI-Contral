## Overview

Products subject to our policy on Knives and Other Weapons must also adhere to other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%ahttps://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview) including our policy on [Firearms](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Firearms) and [Firearm Accessories](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Firearm-accessories), [Firearm Ammunition](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Firearm-ammunition), and [Game Guns](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Prohibited-Products-Policy:-Air-powered-guns,-BB-guns,-toy-guns-and-imitation-firearms) or Walmart will automatically unpublish your item.

## What is the policy?

Walmart prohibits weapons or destructive devices of any kind on the Marketplace. This includes but is not limited to knives and other implements marketed or designed for fighting, combat or self-defense use. Products subject to our policy on Knives and Other Weapons must also adhere to all other [Prohibited Products Policies](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Prohibited%20products%20&%20brands/Prohibited-products-policy:-overview).

## Knives & bladed weapons

### What is the policy?

Walmart prohibits knives and bladed implements designed or intended to be used as weapons. Manual folding knives may not be allowed in certain states/jurisdictions. Most fixed-blade, utility and culinary knives are allowed, unless marketed for fighting, combat or self-defense use.

| Prohibited | Allowed with restriction | Allowed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| - Automatic knives<br>- Ballistic knives<br>- Battle axes<br>- Bayonets<br>- Boot knives<br>- Butterfly knives<br>- Cane swords<br>- Claws or spikes that attach to the shoe, other clothing item or any body part.<br>- Daggers<br>- Flipper knives<br>- Gravity knives<br>- Hidden/disguised knives, such as belt buckle, pen, carabiner and lipstick knives<br>- Karambits<br>- Neck knives<br>- Out-the-front (OTF) knives<br>- Pantera claws<br>- Push daggers<br>- Spearheads<br>- Stiletto knives<br>- Switchblades<br>- Throwing axes<br>- Throwing cards<br>- Throwing knives<br>- Throwing stars | Any marketplace seller who sells and fulfills these items must comply with state-specific laws:<br>- Assisted-open knives<br>- Manual folding knives | - Culinary/kitchen knives<br>- Decorative swords<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Fencing equipment such as foils, sabers and épées<br>- Fixed blade knives for purposes such as hunting/camping/fishing<br>- Hatchets<br>- Multitools<br>- Utility knives |

## Martial arts weapons

### What is the policy?

Walmart prohibits martial arts weapons on the Marketplace. Certain training/practice/plastic or foam items may be allowed.
