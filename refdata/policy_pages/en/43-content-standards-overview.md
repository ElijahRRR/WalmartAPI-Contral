## Overview

Content is vital to the success of any business, especially an e-commerce business. You can help drive sales by providing complete and consistently high-quality product content. Walmart has developed style guides to help you comply with Walmart Content Standards, so customers can continue to enjoy a seamless shopping experience on Walmart.com.

**Notes:**

AI-generated content must be accurate, truthful, rights-compliant, and aligned with Marketplace policies, including our Product details policy.

## What is the policy?

A style guide is a document that specifies the standards you should follow for content creation. Content style guides include information on imagery, copy, search engine optimization and more. Check out the content standards below before adding new items to your Marketplace catalog or before updating existing content.
